How to interpret it

  • Analytic adjusted p-values are Holm-adjusted across the factor tests, and within each comparison family; resampling p-values are Holm-adjusted separately.
  • Primary comparison conclusions use pointwise parametric-bootstrap intervals; analytic p-values and intervals are sensitivity results. Omnibus factor tests are secondary.
  • Confidence intervals are not adjusted for multiple comparisons.
  • A result without clear evidence does not mean the effect is zero.
  • Results across separate metrics are exploratory.

Multiplicity families are formed by model term (analytic factor tests together; resampling checks together) and by each factor or factor-pair comparison family. Unavailable p-values are omitted from Holm adjustment and receive no conclusion; this missing-test policy requires statistical review.

“Analytic result” means the analytic Holm-adjusted p-value is below 0.05; it does not use the resampling or bootstrap results.

Analytic intervals are pointwise 95%; bootstrap intervals are pointwise 95%. Neither is multiplicity-adjusted. Interval agreement compares whether both exclude the null value.

Separate metrics are not corrected together; cross-metric findings are exploratory. A result without clear evidence does not show that an effect is zero, and statistical evidence is not practical importance.
